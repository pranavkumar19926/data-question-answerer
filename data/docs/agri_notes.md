# Agriculture notes (related documents for hybrid stretch goal)

## Seasons
Kharif crops are sown with the monsoon (June-July) and harvested in autumn. In this demo: Rice, Cotton, Soybean are Kharif. Rabi crops are sown in winter (Oct-Nov) and harvested in spring. In this demo: Wheat is Rabi. Sugarcane is an Annual crop taking 12 months.

## States and regions
North: Punjab, Uttar Pradesh. West: Maharashtra. East: West Bengal. South: Tamil Nadu. Central: Madhya Pradesh. Punjab and Uttar Pradesh lead wheat; West Bengal leads rice in this demo.

## How to read production
production_tonnes is total output. area_ha is sown area. Yield = production_tonnes / area_ha. Totals use SUM, averages use AVG. Years covered: 2021-2022 only.
